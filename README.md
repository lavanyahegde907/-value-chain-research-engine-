# Value Chain Research Engine — Task 1

## Farmer & Production Side

### Project
SIP Project — Value Chain Research Engine

### Current Milestone
Milestone 1: Research and Ideation

### Member 1 Focus
This repository contains research for the **farmer and production side** of an agricultural value chain.

The boundary of this task is:

**Production → Growing → Farm-level activities → Harvesting → Crop ready to leave the farm**

This task does **not** cover farmer interviews. Interviews belong to Task 2.

## Main Research Question
> What problems does the farmer face from production until the crop is ready to leave the farm?

## What we need to understand
- Who are the farmers/producers?
- What crop/commodity is being studied?
- How is the crop produced and harvested?
- What labour is needed?
- What machinery/equipment is needed?
- What does labour or machinery availability cost the farmer?
- What seasonal or climate-related problems occur?
- What causes delays, extra cost, or crop loss?
- What happens when the crop is ready to leave the farm?

## Four Required Outputs
1. Problem Brief
2. Stakeholder Map
3. Open Questions
4. Research Log

## Important rule
Do not present assumptions as findings. Every important claim should be supported by a source in the research log.
